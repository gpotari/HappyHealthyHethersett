Privacy & cookies

How this website uses your information

This page explains the small amount of information the Happy Healthy Hethersett website stores so the site can work properly, especially when you sign in to manage events, litter picks, or reports.

Last updated: 13 May 2026

Cookies and local storage

We do not currently use analytics, advertising cookies, marketing pixels, or third-party tracking tools. The storage we use is there to keep account features working after you sign in.

hhh-admin

Secure sign-in cookie used by the API to recognise a logged-in user.

Strictly necessary

hhh.auth.token

Local browser storage that keeps your sign-in active when you refresh or reopen the website.

Strictly necessary

hhh.auth.user

Local browser storage containing basic account details, such as your name, email, roles, and avatar preview.

Strictly necessary

Because these are used for login and account security, we treat them as strictly necessary. You can remove them by signing out or clearing your browser storage, although account features may stop working until you sign in again.

Account Information

If you register or sign in, we store the details needed to manage your account: your name, email address, account role, password hash, avatar image if you upload one, and basic account timestamps.

We never store your password in plain text. Passwords are hashed before they are saved.

Forms, Reports And Photos

If you submit a contact message, litter report, litter pick update, image, location, or event detail, we store the information you choose to send so volunteers can respond, organise work, and keep the website up to date.

Please avoid uploading photos or notes that include people, private addresses, or sensitive details unless they are needed for the report.

Your Choices

You can ask us to update or remove account information where appropriate. You can also sign out at any time to clear the website's saved sign-in state on this device.

If we add analytics or other non-essential tracking in the future, we will ask for consent before turning it on.
